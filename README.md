# Farm Crop & Equipment Management System

## Overview

This system helps manage:

- Crops – crop details, season, expected/actual yield, area  
- Agricultural Equipment – tractors, harvesters, etc., with status and rent details  
- Equipment Bookings – booking equipment for workers/farmers with dates and cost  
- Workers / Farmers – roles, wages, days worked  
- Seeds & Fertilizers (Inventory) – stock quantity, cost, low-stock tracking  
- Expenses & Income – transactions and monthly finance summary  
- Crop Production Records – harvest details, yield, revenue  

The project demonstrates practical use of:
- Arrays– for crops, workers, inventory, monthly finance  
- Linked Lists – for equipment, bookings, transactions, production records  

## Features

- Menu-driven console interface  
- Add, update, delete, and search records for all modules  
- Equipment availability tracking and booking management  
- Basic financial reports (income, expense, profit/loss)  
- Crop-wise production and revenue tracking  
- Clear separation of modules for easy understanding and extension  

## Tech Stack

- Language: C++ (C++11 or later)  
- Data Structures: Arrays, Linked Lists  
- Platform: Any C++ compiler (g++, MinGW , VS Code, etc.)  

## Project Structure 

- `main.cpp` – menu and program entry point  
- `crop.cpp` – crop management functions  
- `equipment.cpp` – equipment and booking management  
- `worker.cpp` – worker/farmer management  
- `inventory.cpp` – seeds & fertilizers management  
- `finance.cpp` – expenses, income, and reports  
- `production.cpp` – crop production records
